# Existing ecosystem and evidence

Research date: 27 October 2026. The official SIH portal returned 403. The community archive is provisional, not official verification. The `www` iGOT AI page timed out; the non-`www` version produced no extractable body. Search indexing and the government PIB release independently establish existing AI capabilities.

| A: Explicit requirement | B: Publicly documented iGOT capability | C: Nexus enhancement | D: Evidence and limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Competency assessment and gaps | AI-CBP analyzes roles/documents and identifies gaps | Statistical-role evidence passport with uncertainty and versioned practical evidence | PIB 16 February 2026; Nexus engine/tests. No claim iGOT lacks evidence features |
| Personalized pathways | AI-CBP and recommendation engine already create role-based pathways | Deterministic explanation combining reference gaps, prerequisites, duration and both catalogue types | PIB; engine and fixtures. Live catalogue matching is pending |
| Virtual assistance | AI Sarthi and in-course AI Tutor exist | Scoped statistical-document retrieval with visible citations and bounded provider adapter | PIB; Nexus AI tests. No live provider validation yet |
| Multilingual learning | Transcription/subtitles already documented | English/Hindi navigation and honest untranslated-content notices | PIB; browser tests. Full translation not complete |
| NSSTA TPAC programmes | Separate NSSTA training governance | Joint catalogue provenance and explicit approval-evidence field | NSSTA document listing; no programme approvals imported |
| Uploaded-material quizzes | Assessments already part of platform context | Exact-excerpt checks, trainer approval, immutable published snapshots | User brief/archive; SQL/AI tests. Factual quality still requires expert review |
| Learner/admin analytics | Organizational capacity-building tools already exist | Before/after evidence and transparent assumption-based statistical workforce simulator | PIB; browser demonstration. Not a validated forecast |

CBC and Karmayogi Bharat jointly presented the tools. MoSPI provides the official statistical domain; NSSTA provides statistical training. The proposed difference is the assembled statistical competency-to-practice lifecycle, not invention of AI mapping, tutoring or recommendations.
