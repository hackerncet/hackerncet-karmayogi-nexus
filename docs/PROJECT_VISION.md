# Project vision

Help professionals in the Official Statistical System choose training relevant to their work and demonstrate the resulting capability. Complement iGOT and NSSTA, with no official endorsement claim.

The competency-to-impact engine connects job role → required proficiency → baseline evidence → gaps → recommendations → learning → practical application → post-assessment → evidence history → authorized aggregate insight. Completion, evidence, confidence and simulated impact remain distinct.

First delivery is a working isolated demonstration and tested domain/security foundations. A real organizational rollout additionally needs authorized data, governance approval, real provider validation, SSO, retention processes and live end-to-end testing. No novelty exclusivity, causality or government compliance is asserted.
