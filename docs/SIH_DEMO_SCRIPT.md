# SIH demonstration walkthrough

Start `npm run dev`, open `/`, then **Explore the live demo**. All records are synthetic and actions simulated. For repeatability, reset at `/demo/settings` before beginning.

1. Inspect the independent-platform disclaimer and illustrative passport.
2. Enter `/demo/dashboard`; inspect Ananya Rao’s statistical officer profile via `/demo/profile`.
3. Open `/demo/diagnostic`; start and complete six questions. Multiple-answer items require all correct options. Submission updates evidence, not a government certification.
4. Inspect `/demo/skill-gaps`; show target, estimate, gap, confidence and priority. Open passport evidence details. Highlight GIS/metadata unknowns rather than zero scores.
5. Open `/demo/recommendations`; explain the rationale and provider provenance. Filter for both iGOT and NSSTA synthetic entries. Show the missing TPAC approval evidence and unscheduled dates explicitly.
6. Join a demo path, open `/demo/learning-paths`, continue the lesson and mark it complete. Show that completion alone does not update proficiency.
7. Enter `/demo/practice-labs/quality`; flag missing income, duplicate identifier, inconsistent case, implausible age and extreme income. Evaluate and inspect new practical evidence. Outliers require review, not automatic deletion.
8. Optional: sampling mission asks weight 1/0.2=5 and weighted mean (10×1+30×4)/5=26; SQL mission uses fixed grammar only.
9. Open Trainer studio, upload a nonsensitive TXT/Markdown file. Demonstrate that the file stays in this browser and real AI remains unavailable.
10. In question bank, choose built-in Sampling essentials and load three **prewritten demo examples**. Do not call this real AI generation.
11. Preview each question; inspect its source excerpt and answer; edit/review and approve all three.
12. Open assessment builder and publish the reviewed demonstration quiz. Open `/demo/assessments/published` and complete it.
13. Show recalculated passport estimates and `/demo/my-progress`. Explain that small coverage and repeated questions limit inference.
14. Ask the demonstration assistant about sampling or weights. Show its prewritten label and source, then clear the chat.
15. Open `/demo/admin`; filter synthetic departments. Explain that chart values are calculated from deterministic fixtures and available in a table.
16. Open the impact simulator. Default: population 200, participation 80%, completion 85%, effectiveness 50%, baseline coverage 30%, 10 hours. Baseline projects 160 participants, 136 completers, 1,600 hours and 53.8% coverage.
17. Change effectiveness and compare conservative/baseline/best-case projections. Export assumptions. Say **simulation, not a forecast or causal proof**.
18. Show integration status and the test/alignment reports. Clearly separate local demonstration, unverified configured backend, missing AI credentials and blocked government APIs.

The browser test `complete competency-to-impact demonstration` reproduces the central sequence. Its fixture answers are test data, not a protected official assessment bank.
